B.Tech Mechanical Engineering: Scheme of Studies 2024-25
Total credits: 160

Semester I

Course Code: BT101
Course Title: Engineering Biology (3-0-0, 3 credits)
Unit 1: Introduction to life (8 hours) - Cell structure, prokaryotes and eukaryotes, biomolecules
Unit 2: Genetics (8 hours) - Mendel's laws, DNA structure, DNA replication, genetic code
Unit 3: Biology for engineers (8 hours) - Biosensors, bioinspired design, biomaterials, bioenergy

MA101 Engineering Mathematics I
Unit I Matrices: rank of a matrix, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, Cayley-Hamilton theorem, diagonalisation
Unit II Differential calculus: Taylor series, partial derivatives, maxima and minima
Unit III Integral calculus: double integrals, triple integrals, change of variables

PH101 Applied Physics (3-1-0)
Unit 1: Quantum physics - Wave-particle duality, Schrodinger equation, particle in a box
Unit 2: Electrostatics - Coulomb's law, Gauss's law, electric potential, capacitors
Unit 3: Waves and optics - Interference, diffraction, polarisation

Semester II

ME201 Engineering Mechanics
Unit 1: Force systems: resultant of forces, equilibrium of particles, free body diagrams
Unit 2: Friction: laws of friction, wedge friction, ladder problems
Unit 3: Kinematics: projectile motion, relative motion

HS201 Indian Knowledge System
Unit 1: Introduction: Vedic literature, Indian philosophy, Indian mathematics and astronomy
Unit 2: Applications: Ayurveda, Indian architecture, Indian metallurgy
